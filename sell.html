§ Sell your books

Have a
collection?

We're always looking to buy. Single titles, a shelf, or a whole collection — if it overlaps with what we stock, we'd like to hear about it.

§ 01 — What we're after

The same things
we stock.

If you'd find it in the catalogue, we're probably interested.

The short list: twentieth-century books on UFOs, the paranormal, parapsychology, cryptozoology, ghost-and-haunting literature, ley lines and earth mysteries, Western esoterica, and Forteana generally. Mass-market paperbacks, hardbacks, magazines, the occasional pamphlet.

Condition matters but doesn't need to be pristine — we stock honest reading copies, so we buy honest reading copies. We pass on anything heavily damaged: water-staining, missing pages, broken bindings.

If you're not sure whether it fits, send a rough list and we'll give you a straight answer.

§ 02 — How it works

Pretty simple.

  1. 01

    Tell us
    what you have.

    Use the form below. A rough list is fine to start — a dozen titles or a hundred, whichever you have to hand. Photographs of the shelf are useful but not required.

  2. 02

    We'll get
    back to you.

    Usually within a few days. Either with an offer, or with questions, or — if it's not something we can take on — an honest steer toward somewhere that might.

  3. 03

    If we agree,
    we pay.

    Bank transfer on receipt. We cover postage for anything we've committed to buy. For larger collections we can work something out — for the right haul, collection is possible.
