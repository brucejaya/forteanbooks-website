§ Got books to sell?

Selling a
collection?

I'm always looking to buy. Single titles, a shelf, or a whole collection — if it overlaps with what I sell, I'd like to hear about it.

§ 01 — What I'm after

The same things
I sell.

If you'd see it on the catalogue page here, I'd probably like to buy it.

The short list: twentieth-century books on UFOs, the paranormal, parapsychology, cryptozoology, ghost-and-haunting literature, ley lines and earth mysteries, Western esoterica, and Forteana generally. Mass-market paperbacks, hardbacks, magazines, the occasional pamphlet.

Condition matters but doesn't need to be pristine — I sell honest reading copies, so I happily buy honest reading copies. I do tend to pass on books that are heavily damaged, water-stained, missing pages, or with broken bindings.

If you're not sure whether what you have is in scope, just send a rough list. I'll tell you honestly.

§ 02 — How it works

Pretty simple.

  1. 01

    Tell me
    what you have.

    Use the form below. A rough list is fine to start — a dozen titles or a hundred, whichever you have to hand. Photographs of the shelf are useful but not required.

  2. 02

    I'll get
    back to you.

    Usually within a few days. Either with an offer, or with questions, or — if it's not for me — an honest "this isn't really my patch, here's who I'd try instead."

  3. 03

    If we agree,
    I'll pay.

    Bank transfer on receipt. I'll cover postage to me for anything I've committed to buy. Larger collections we can work out — sometimes I can come and collect.
