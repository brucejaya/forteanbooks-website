W. Raymond Drake's meticulous survey of aerial phenomena and spaceman encounters across world mythologies and ancient civilisations. First published 1968, this 1970 edition. Drake worked independently of von Däniken and arguably with greater scholarly rigour — his cross-cultural approach to ancient astronaut evidence is systematic and thorough.

An increasingly rare title. Drake is undervalued relative to his influence on the genre.
