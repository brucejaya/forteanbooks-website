Guy Lyon Playfair's exploration of the boundary between the physical and paranormal — drawing on his years of fieldwork in Brazil investigating poltergeist phenomena, psychic surgeons, and spirit possession. Panther paperback, 1977.

Playfair later wrote the definitive account of the Enfield Poltergeist and carried out extensive investigations for the Society for Psychical Research. This earlier work shows the foundations of that rigorous approach. A serious entry in British paranormal literature.
