Paul Thomas (pen name of French composer and intellectual Paul Misraki) surveys aerial phenomena across recorded history — from biblical accounts and classical sources to modern sightings. Published 1973. Originally published in France as Les Extraterrestres, this English edition reached a wider audience.

Misraki's cultural and historical breadth makes this a more rigorous treatment than most books of the genre. A scarce and underrated title well worth seeking out.
