Jadoo by John A. Keel — the debut book from the author of The Mothman Prophecies and The Eighth Tower. 1958 UK first edition, published by W.H. Allen in the same year as the US Messner first. Complete with original dustjacket in good condition with minor edge wear.

Keel spent years travelling through India and the Middle East investigating fakirs, rope-trick performers, snake charmers, and practitioners of black magic — this is his first-hand account of what he found. A remarkable book, entirely unlike his later UFO work, and considerably rarer.
